Key Considerations

⚠️ 33% Threshold

No single entity should control more than 33% of staked ETH. Lido's share (~27.7%) is watched closely, though its stake is distributed across 37+ node operators.

🔄 Pectra Upgrade

Raised validator stake cap from 32 to 2,048 ETH, enabling more efficient large-scale participation and reducing validator sprawl.

📉 Yield Compression

APY has declined from post-Merge highs of 5.3% to ~1.9-3% as more ETH is staked. MEV-Boost remains crucial for competitive yields.
