## C. Show notes (paste into Spotify / YouTube description)

**The DAO Hack, 10 Years On | Then / Now, from The Bitcoin Podcast archive**

In 2016 the DAO raised about 12 million ether, roughly 15% of all ether in existence, and then lost a third of it to a single reentrancy bug. We play what Emin Gün Sirer and the TBP hosts said ten days after the hack, then check it against what happened next: the hard fork, Ethereum Classic, the SEC's DAO Report, the alleged identity of the attacker, and why the same bug class still costs DeFi tens of millions.
