## Episode Description

In this episode, we interview Kacper Bak, Research Engineer at Quanstamp. Quantstamp has created a system for auditing smart contracts automatically, testing for known exploits. They verify the more common attack vectors, and provide a report to the user about the potential threat. We go into how their product is built, the future of their product, and how it can benefit and drive future innovation. **Links:** – [smart contract security alliance](https://www.smartcontractsecurityalliance.com/) – [secureth community](https://secureth.org) – [quantstamp website](https://quantstamp.com/)
