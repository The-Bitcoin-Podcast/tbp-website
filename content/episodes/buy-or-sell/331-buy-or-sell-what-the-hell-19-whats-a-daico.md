## Episode Description

In this episode of Buy or Sell, JJ, Alec, Dee, and Chinmay bring the new hotness that is the DAICO into the topic of conversation. Chinmay is the Chief Technical Officer of the Rhythm Network, and he is excited about the future where DAICOs are the new norm. We define it, and explore what it could offer to markets as an investment vehicle. We shortly discus how entrenched players are losing their talent to the crypto world, and, we play a game where we each guess what Coinbase will add next. What will they add next?
