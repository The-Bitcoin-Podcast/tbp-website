## Episode Description

In Episode 39 of The Bull Pen Podcast, The Crypto Bully steps into the Bull Pen with Matthias Jakisch. Matthias is a serial entrepreneur with a background in software engineering as well as owning and running a software company. This experience combined with his knowledge and enthusiasm for blockchain and cryptocurrency is what set the stage for the launching of MarkNetwork, a blockchain solutions enterprise. Matthias speaks with Lyndon about the many big things MarkNetwork has in the works and isn’t shy about letting his compassion be heard as you’ll be able to tell throughout this interview.
