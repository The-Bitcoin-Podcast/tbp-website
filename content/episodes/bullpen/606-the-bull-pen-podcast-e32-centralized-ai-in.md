## Episode Description

In Episode 32 of The Bull Pen Podcast, The Crypto Bully steps into the Bull Pen with one of the Top 8 Women in Blockchain, Jennifer Greyson. Jen is a CEO, author, international speaker and serves as a board member for Kerala Blockchain Academy. She also brings to crypto 20+ years of experience building and maintaining businesses across a multitude of industries including Blockchain and Artificial Intelligence. Jen hits on many important and interesting topics during her chat with Lyndon including revisiting the early days of AOL during the dot com bubble, advice for women new to the blockchain and cryptocurrency space and how scary the thought of centralized AI can really be.
