## Episode Description

In Episode 27 of The Bull Pen Podcast, The Crypto Bully steps into the Bull Pen with DJsNeverEndingStory. DJ, originally from St. Louis, Missouri, he is the host of the Crypto Until Infinity Podcast and a crypto enthusiast. DJ’s achievements include a Top 10 Billboard ambient album, (The Harmony Epoch), role as composer for an animated series with the creators of It’s Always Sunny in Philadelphia, (Unsupervised), production for the NBA, numerous collaborations with spoken word artist Prince Ea, and a triumphant story of overcoming brief homelessness in LA to entrepreneurship by investing in cryptocurrency. DJ tells all in this episode and goes into detail about how he got into the cryptocurrency and blockchain space as well as the lesson he learned from losing out on $1.2 million in crypto.
