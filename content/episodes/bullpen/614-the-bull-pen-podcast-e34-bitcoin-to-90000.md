## Episode Description

In Episode 34 of The Bull Pen Podcast, The Crypto Bully steps into the Bull Pen with Jeremy Horton (CEO) and Stephen Harano (COO Lead Analyst) of Block Society. Block Society has rightfully gained the title of the first SEC licensed and registered cryptocurrency financial advising team. What exactly does this mean? Extra protection for the consumer and no annoying disclaimer saying, “This is not financial advice.” Jeremy and Stephen go into detail with Lyndon on what it took to accomplish this with the SEC and the battles they still face even after obtaining this status. They also break down the fundamentals and technical analysis on where they believe Bitcoin and the whole cryptocurrency market is headed soon.
