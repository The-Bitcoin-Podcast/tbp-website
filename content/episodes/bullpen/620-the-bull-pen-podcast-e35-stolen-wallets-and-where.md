## Episode Description

In Episode 35 of The Bull Pen Podcast, The Crypto Bully steps into the Bull Pen with Rafael Hauxley also known as The Bitcoin Traveler. The reason he has this nickname is because Rafael managed to travel around the world in 12 months visiting 20 countries and capturing 178 hours of footage including 28 interviews...on a single Bitcoin! Rafael shares the details of this amazing journey with Lyndon and talks about discovering a market when one could purchase stolen wallets still containing the original owner’s belongings and sneaking in to meet the creator of Ethereum, Vitalik Buterin. He also speaks on the documentary he is currently developing showcasing his travels around the world, and his new book “Stolen Wallets and Where to Find Them” which includes a word puzzle wallet prize in Bitcoin that is still up for grabs.
