## Episode Description

In Episode 24 of The Bull Pen Podcast, The Crypto Bully steps into the Bull Pen with Dwayne Mooney. Dwayne is a comedian, writer, social critic, and television and film actor best known for his role in the movie “The Fish That Saved Pittsburg” and the 2016 Bravo television and reality docuseries “Newlyweds: The First Year.” He is also the son of the famous comedian Paul Mooney who is best known for writing for Richard Prior and his appearances on “Chappelle’s Show.” In this episode Dwayne speaks on how he got into cryptocurrency, his experience with trading Forex and other interesting topics.
