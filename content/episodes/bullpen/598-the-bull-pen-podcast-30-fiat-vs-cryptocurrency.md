## Episode Description

In Episode 30 of The Bull Pen Podcast, The Crypto Bully steps into the Bull Pen with Marcello, Corey and Demetrick. These 3 gentlemen are the founders of The Bitcoin Podcast Network and the hosts of multiple podcasts both on and off their own network. Between all of them they have an array of skills including graphic design, marketing and security engineering (just to name a few) and are highly active within the blockchain and cryptocurrency space. Marcello, Corey and Demetrick share with Lyndon the early days of The Bitcoin Podcast Network, how The Bull Pen Podcast came to be a part of their lineup and then have an interesting debate on how cryptocurrency stacks up against fiat currency.
