## Episode Description

In Episode 8 of The Bull Pen Podcast, The Crypto Bully steps into the Bull Pen with Tyler Sanford, former major league baseball player for the Los Angeles Angels turned ICO, blockchain and marketing management consultant. Even though this is Tyler’s first podcast appearance, this episode is full of dropped gems with all the experience he brings to the table from the ICO world. Listen to Tyler speak on how he chooses projects to work with, the top three things projects should be focused on, his best source for finding investors for ICOs and his take on community-based cryptocurrency projects.
