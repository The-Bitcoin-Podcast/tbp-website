## Episode Description

In Episode 37 of The Bull Pen Podcast, The Crypto Bully steps back into the Bull Pen with Bitcoin Ben. Back at it again, Lyndon and Ben can’t help but let the good times roll with a conversation full of jokes, laughter and cryptocurrency. This go around Ben shares his opinions on Craig Wright claiming to be Satoshi Nakamoto, the current market and where he believes it’s headed in the future and what you might find him doing 25 years from now. And of course, they couldn’t go a full episode without mentioning Litecoin.
