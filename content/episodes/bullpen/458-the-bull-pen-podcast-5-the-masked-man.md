## Episode Description

In Episode 5 of The Bull Pen Podcast, The Crypto Bully steps into the Bull Pen with blockchain and cryptocurrency influencer Shill Nye. This masked man is no noob to the crypto scene, is a known trader and speaks on where he’s had the most success in trading crypto and the strategies he likes to use. He also discusses why he decides to cover his face to conceal his identity, his opinions on the crypto Twitter world and what he looks forward to the most within crypto. Shill Nye also shares about traveling around the world to spread awareness of himself and his company Elevated District and the cool video content he is currently working on revolving around the crypto lifestyle.
