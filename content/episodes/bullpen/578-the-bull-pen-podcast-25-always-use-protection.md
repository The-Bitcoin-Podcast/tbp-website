## Episode Description

In Episode 25 of The Bull Pen Podcast, The Crypto Bully steps into the Bull Pen with trader and technical analyst Brain Swan. With over 20 years of experience as a Certified Financial Technician (CFTe), Brian brings with him to crypto a high level of expertise within the realm of technical analysis. Brain hits on the difference in charting larger liquidity cryptos (i.e. Bitcoin, Litecoin, etc.) vs smaller alts, the importance of risk management when learning how to trade and how human psychology and emotional control plays into it all. Brian, along side Paul McNeal (news curator) and Bobby Atkins (technical analyst), also co-host a weekly live show via YouTube called “Support and Resistance” that combines the fundamental/news and the technical all in one place.
