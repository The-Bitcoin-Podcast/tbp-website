## Episode Description

Wills on the blockchain? Felipe and I talk about how Legacy Network aims to create a solution to sending your digital assets to loved ones when you die, including your (encrypted) private keys. They hope to integrate in the future for offchain assets as well. Their solution could create a trustless way to bequeath property and memories for a much cheaper price and at your convenience. It can also be a solution for sending messages not just on passing but on birthdays and anniversaries. Listen to the episode and find out how.
