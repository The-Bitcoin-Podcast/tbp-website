## Episode Description

This week Lucian and Collin discuss various news worthy topics in the Ethereum space. Since DeFi applications are dependent on one another, can their interactions lead to contagion? Hopper is a zero knowledge mixer, we look at some other attempts at bringing privacy to Ethereum and how they compare. Grid+ released a layer 2 solution using their 'SafeCards.'
