## Episode Description

Collin and Lucian discuss the Ethereum gateway announced by Cloudflare this week. While it works in a similar way to Infura, this shows how the large developer community is attracting established players. Oxfam rolled out a pilot program that give South Pacific Islanders access to Dai tokens in short notice in case of a natural disaster. Lastly we talk about a EthSites which encodes data directly into the Ethereum blockchain for censorship resistance.
