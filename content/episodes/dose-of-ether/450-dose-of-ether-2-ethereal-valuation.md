## Episode Description

Thanks for joining Bijan and Lucian on another episode of "Dose of Ether." We talk about the false dichotomy of scaling layer 1 versus layer 2, and why both should be explored without favoritism (for now). Will ETH go to zero? No, and I am willing to put money on it. Coinbase lays out a vision of a user controlled internet, and Aragon shows us how a personal DAO can help you manage this new control.
