## Episode Description

Lucian is joined by Giuseppe Littera, the Co-Founder of Sardex. Sardex is a mutual credit agency that allows small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) to trade local goods and services with one another using their centralized platform to keep a ledger of transactions. After over 10 years of operation there are 4,000 SMEs participating in the network with over 1,000 employees of these companies being partly compensated in Sardex. They've successfully done the community building that many crypto companies still hope to achieve.
