## Episode Description

Today is a fun one. I first talked to Esther a few years ago about her stand up and acting career and, well, what do you know? It's a small world! I caught wind that she was into crypto and wanted to know if she wanted to come on our show to talk shop! Comedian's Rosie Tran and Esther Ku launch the "Hello Crypto Kitty" Podcast to discuss all things related to Cryptocurrency, Bitcoin, and Altcoins. The podcast is currently available on Periscope Live and Youtube. In addition, the boys talk about a variety of issues and we're joined by our slack friend Collin Cusce.
