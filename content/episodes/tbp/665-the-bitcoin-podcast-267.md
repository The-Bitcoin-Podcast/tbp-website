## Episode Description

In this episode The 3 Amigos of Crypto talk about the value of time, and how streaming money can fundamentally change the employee--employer relationship. We also interview Sam Radoccia, the author of The Bitcoin Pizza Book. She presents a precedent that there is a lot of "bullshit" in crypto and her book and poise aims to identify, isolate and remove the bullshit to provide a clearer representation of what crypto has to offer. Also...is Justin Timberlake a good actor? We find out in this episode. And, Netflix...because why not talk about that business model on a cryptocurrency podcast?!
