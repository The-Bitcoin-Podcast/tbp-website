## Episode Description

We are very privileged to present Péter Szilágyi and Martin Swende of Ethereum to discuss the 1.9.0 release of the Geth Ethereum client. This release is chocked full of huge quality of life improvements as well as new features. We delve deep into the process for producing this release, what these features are, what they mean going forward,, and speak about the impact of Ethereum 2.0 on the Geth client. It's a great honor having them donate their time to discuss their work, and it's a fantastic conversation.
