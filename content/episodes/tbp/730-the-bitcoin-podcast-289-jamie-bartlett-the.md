## Episode Description

In this episode of The Bitcoin Podcast, Corey and Dee interview Jamie Bartlett. Bartlett presented the tragic story of Onecoin. A ponzi scheme that took advantage of millions of people and resulted in billions of dollars being fraudulently removed from their owners. Dr Ruja Ignatova persuaded millions to join her financial revolution. Then she disappeared. Why? Jamie Bartlett presents a story of greed, deceit and herd madness.
