## Episode Description

In Episode 26 of The Bull Pen Podcast, The Crypto Bully steps into the Bull Pen with CoinGecko’s Co-Founder Bobby Ong. CoinGecko is one the world’s leading cryptocurrency data analytics and research platforms, and Bobby goes into detail with Lyndon about what makes their website such a great resource for both newbs and novices. Bobby speaks on what it takes to create and continuously run a resource of this size, how they stay ahead of the competition and his thoughts on the current state of the cryptocurrency market.
