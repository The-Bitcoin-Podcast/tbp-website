## Episode Description

Lots of news this week! Litecoin Foundation Acquires 9.9% of Bank in Payments Partnership and Coinbase is adding alts. Which exactly? Well, they are exploring Cardano, Basic Attention Token, Stellar Lumens, Zcash, and 0x. If you’re holding any of those bags, this is probably a swell week for you. In addition, Clay Collins, who is one of the long time internet marketers, joins us this week in the studio. His new startup Nomics.com is aiming to be the Yahoo! Finance for crypto, so we invited him on to geek out. Also, he has his own podcast. Published Every Thursday, Flippening Gives You… Conversations with top analysts and technicians, news roundtables with preeminent crypto journalists and deep dives with fund managers.
