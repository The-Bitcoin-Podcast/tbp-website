## Episode Description

In this episode Dee and Dr. Petty discuss the merits of using cryptocurrency as a payment system. This naturally leads to a discussion about scaling the Bitcoin Blockchain. Can crypto scale? That is the question. Also, Tim Cook doesn't like blockchains too much, let alone cryptocurrency. Well, Dee has some news for Mr. Cook about how the ole Federal Reserve functions. This week's interview is Nicolas Dorier. Nicolas has been a Bitcoin developer on Bitcoin Core and is the CEO of a BTCPay Server. BTCPay Server is an open-source payment processor to challenge BitPay in the Bitcoin payment market.
