## Episode Description

Lucian talks with Nick Fett, the CEO of DAXIA, a decentralized derivatives platform. Having worked at the CFTC prior to starting his company, he brings a wealth of insights into the market he is trying to disrupt. We debate if decentralized finance is regulatory arbitrage or an attempt at radical markets.
