## Episode Description

This week we get into a topical debate on adoption. Cello bring up the fact that when you talk about 'normies' you just further isolate your cause. Most people are 'General Purpose People (GPP's)' and they are not using crypto because it is inconvenient and pointless. So, here we are at the 10 year mark. Are you satisfied with it's progress? If you want crypto to be mass adopted, then you better listen to what all those 'normies' are saying and stop acting like a fringe club for anarchists. We look at this debate from many different viewpoints and open up the call lines for our fans to call in to join the discussion!
