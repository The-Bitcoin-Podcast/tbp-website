## Episode Description

In this episode the Three Amigos of Crypto have a roundtable discussion about the viral Trump tweets about Bitcoin and Cryptocurrency. Wayne joins us as we open up the phone lines and Cello discusses Trumps take on Bitcoin and Libra. Also, Dee has a message for the half-assed crypto enthusiast. Dr. Corey Petty also gives everyone the results of a Twitter poll that was designed to separate the knows and the know-nots when it comes to the subtlety of how this blockchain technology works.
