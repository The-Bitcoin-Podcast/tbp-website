## Episode Description

Swan is an application that allows you to buy bitcoin through your bank account in small amounts and create a Bitcoin Savings Account automatically. The boys bring on Brady Swenson, Chief Education Officer of Swan to discuss how this works and why someone would want to use this. During the roundtable, Dee and Corey discuss the concepts of "wrapped BTC" (wBTC) and "liquid BTC" and their differences. Corey then goes on to do a technical explainer on how to deploy smart contracts, and some things to watch out for. Enjoy the show!
