## Episode Description

Vitalik nerd rages out at a conference! Plus, Kora is building a more inclusive financial system on the blockchain, through infrastructure for community-owned financial services ecosystems. The goal is to provide low-cost, universal access financial services for everyone. We feature Maomao Hu from Kora on todays episode because it is absolutely crucial in bringing financial services to outcasts markets. Providing accessible technology clears the path to literacy and inclusion in terms of financial services. It makes launching a financial services business as cheap and easy as launching a new instance on AWS, with the prime benefit of driving down costs and increasing the variety of offerings for customers.
