## Episode Description

Thanks for joining Bijan and Lucian on another episode of "Dose of Ether." Lucian is back from Berlin with stories about the city and the people who make the EthGlobal events special, like the guys at NewCypher who implemented Fully Homeomorphic Encryption in a smart contract for the first time during the hackathon! Also hear about WebAssembly aka WASM and the future of the EVM, Connexhub and Spankchain implement the first non-custodial payment channel, and the 0x protocol v2 goes live with NTF support.
