## Episode Description

Dee and Corey touch on the bitcoin halving and have a rare discussion on the price of Bitcoin. Dee built an app this week and Corey gives a technical answer for what Deep Work is for everyone. Our guest is CTO Fazri Zubair of Lucid Sight a company that has developed a blockchain game called Crypto Space Commander. This is a MMO style game that launched on Aug 26Th 2019 and is currently in an Alpha release on Steam.
