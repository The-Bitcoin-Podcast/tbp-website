## Episode Description

In Episode 29 of The Bull Pen Podcast, The Crypto Bully steps into the Bull Pen with Jaide Barclay. Jaide had been actively investing in crypto since 2017, and one day decided that she wanted to get more involved in the blockchain and cryptocurrency space. So, what did she do? Hopped on a plane from The Gold Coast, Australia to attend London Blockchain Week at a moment’s notice! It was from that action that she gained the inspiration for creating the 12 Week Crypto Challenge. Jaide goes into deeper detail with Lyndon about exactly how this challenge came about, her plans to travel to 7 countries and 16 cities using nothing but crypto and they both reflect on their earlier days of having “blockfolio syndrome” during the 2017-2018 cryptocurrency bull run.
