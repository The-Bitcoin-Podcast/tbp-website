## Episode Description

The boys zero in on security and talk a bit about the price bouncing back in the market. In addition, Bitcoin (BTC) developer Jimmy Song stops by again so we can get an update on his Bitcoin thoughts, his definition of sound money, and he talks about how he finds new companies to invest in if he believes Bitcoin is already the winner. In February of this year, Song announced an initiative designed to educate and compensate Bitcoin developers.
