## Episode Description

On our roundtable discussion Jessie, Dee, and Corey talked about what is an airdrop and how airdrops incentivize communities. In this interview Corey and Dee have a great conversation with Edan Yago of Sovryn.app. Sovryn.app is a decentralized platform that seeks to allow for Bitcoin Hodlers to have the control they need to participate in various financial instruments with their Bitcoin.
