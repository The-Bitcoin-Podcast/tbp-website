## Episode Description

Our guest this week is Carolyn Reckhow, a Blockchain​ Enthusiast and Director of Operations at ConsenSys​.​ Her mind is a necessary part of the “blockchain 2.0”/Ethereum industry as it has grown from a research project into helping Fortune 500 companies build production blockchain applications. We chat about remedying some of the social and economic inequity issues facing the developing world. With blockchain identity solutions (like uPort for example) there are fascinating possibilities for new business models around putting personal data back into the hands of the people.
