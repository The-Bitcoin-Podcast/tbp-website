## Episode Description

In this episode, the three Amigos of Crypto bring back Bitcoin Zay to discuss his new book, "Bitcoin and Black America." They also take a stroll down memory lane and recall some past trading stories from Dee. Again, Libra is a topic of discussion as it is currently unavoidable. Also, a special 4th host this week, Jessie "The Man" Broke, swings by the show to offer up some of his opinions and what he gleans from the crypto ecosystem.
