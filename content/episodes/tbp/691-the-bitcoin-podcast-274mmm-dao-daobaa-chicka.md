## Episode Description

In this episode, Corey and Dee get a dose of ether from the host of Dose of Ether, Mr. Lucian. What transpires is a sicussion about the dos and don'ts, ins and outs, and pros and cons of DAOs. It has been a mightly long time since The DAO, and the Decentralized Autonomous Organization is on the road to the comeback tour! Celsius Network, MakerDAO, and other organizations are trying their hand in launching this programmatic organization. A business built on code, living on code, or dying by code. Enjoy!
