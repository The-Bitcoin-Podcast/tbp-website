## Episode Description

In this week's episode Corey goes on a spirit hike to elevate his mind body and soul leaving Dee to tag in Wayne (Yes2Crypto) as the Co-Host. The Lightning Network raises the same concerns for Dee that he has had for years. Since 2016 he has worried about the evolution of the middle man and Wayne comes through to ease his mindset. It's all about time. We are still very early. This week's interview is Alex Bosworth. Alex is one of the main engineers on the lightning network infrastructure and co-author of the recent Lightning Loop implementation with Bryan Wu. Lightning Loop seeks to address the liquidity issues with lightning channels. Alex is also a CEO of Yall, a site in which users can be paid using the lightning network.
