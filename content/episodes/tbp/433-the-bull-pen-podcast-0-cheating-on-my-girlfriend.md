## Episode Description

In Episode 0 of The Bull Pen Podcast, Lyndon Morgan II rolls solo and makes sure to show his humorously authentic personality from the second he hops onto the mic. His goal was to create a podcast that was not only informative, but also entertaining by nature which most will find out within the first 3 minutes of listening to Episode 0. Lyndon wants everyone, his guest and listeners of all knowledge and experience levels, to feel equally comfortable by getting know the person behind the microphone on a more personal level. He speaks directly to the listeners giving out the backstory on why he became The Crypto Bully, how The Bull Pen Podcast came about how The Bitcoin Podcast Network and ECC ties in with it all. Three things you can expect to know after listening to this episode: What motivates Lyndon to keep moving forward, How passionate he is about blockchain and cryptocurrency, And what you can expect from The Bull Pen Podcast moving forward. He also tells some good and humorous stories on what it’s like to work on a decentralized crypto team, how he got John McAfee as his first interview and how his girlfriend thought that he was creeping around on her with a woman named Blockfolio.
