## Episode Description

Whoa! Coinbase to Launch its First Crypto Index Fund? We chat about that and also invite Jack Tatar, co-author of the new book, Cryptoassets: The Innovative Investor’s Guide to Bitcoin and Beyond, on the show. Even if you already know most of the concepts on this book, it was very eye opening for little details you could miss out on. He worked in financial services for over two decades, but now Jack is an angel investor and an advisor to startups in the cryptoasset community. Oh, and he is just an all-around great guy. Join us as we unleash the knowledge!
