## Episode Description

In this episode Dee and Dr. Petty bring up the conversation of hardware wallets and personal security. We discuss the pros and cons of hardware wallets and why you should or should not buy one. Are hardware wallets even the final form of paramount crypto security? There is also an interview with an Industrial Engineer, Rick V. He is making building a node and hardware wallet security a thing anyone can do. With very neat, secure, and interesting 3D printed accessories for these two sectors of the Bitcoin space. More people are coming on board and securing their Bitcoin with hardware devices.
