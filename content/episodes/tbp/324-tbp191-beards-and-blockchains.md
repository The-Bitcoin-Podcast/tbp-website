## Episode Description

It’s #BlockchainBeardGuy time! On this episode we talk about the critical roles non-developers play in the blockchain world, amongst a bunch of other current event topics. Don't know who our guest is today? It's almost the perfect personality type for joining our show. He is a passionate decentralized architect and blockchain technologist in Denver, CO. In addition to working with Web 3.0 tech, he loves educating, speaking, and writing about the massive transformative change that's coming our way, and what it all means for business, culture, government, art, and personal relationships. Strap in and be a fly on the wall for our hour-long exchange of ideas and commentary.
