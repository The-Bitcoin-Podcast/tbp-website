## Episode Description

Lucian and Jessie are joined by son and father co-founders George and CEO Dr. Michael Weiksner from Pocketful of Quarters. They've built a way to transfer the in-game currencies between games. They also just became the first ERC-20 token to receive an SEC No-Action letter, meaning that if they meet the requirements set forth they will be recognized as a utility token. Will this be a precedent that other projects follow?
