## Episode Description

n Episode 23 of The Bull Pen Podcast, The Crypto Bully steps into the Bull Pen with Mitch Lacsamana. Mitch serves as the Media & Entertainment Solutions Engineer for Verizon Digital, is the Co-Founder and Director of Operations at Spurantics, a professional brand development agency as well as host of the show “Mitch Does” where he attends conferences and hears the stories of passionate individuals. The awesome thing about where business and networking collides for Mitch is that he gets to sit down with amazing individuals, which of course comes with amazing stories. Mitch shares a few of these crazy yet insightful stories on the podcast that will probably have you on the edge of your seat, the same as Lyndon was, while hearing them.
