## Episode Description

The Three Amigos of Crypto return after a week off to bring you an amazing interview with the one and only, Andreas M. Antonopoulos. We discuss the effect of a long term commitment to the PoW consensus algorithm. We also discuss the most recent experiences from his never-ending tour to educate the world on all things crypto. Corey and Dee get in a lively debate about what will or what wont be in the world of digital currency.
