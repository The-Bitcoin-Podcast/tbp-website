## Episode Description

Dee and Corey go straight into the Interview with Cedric Dahl, an OG of the Bitcoin space, to talk about Bitcoin's dominant (and growing) use on the darknet markets. Cedric runs a think tank called 'The 1000x Group', which monitors crypto adoption to look for early signals of long term value creation. The group studies history's most successful tech investors and applies their learnings to crypto.
