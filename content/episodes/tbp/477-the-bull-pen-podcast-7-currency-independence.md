## Episode Description

In Episode 7 of The Bull Pen Podcast, The Crypto Bully steps into the Bull Pen with Blake Miles, a former member of Team McAfee and Special Forces Green Beret Veteran of the Unites States Army. It's nothing short of interesting on how Blake's military past with unconventional warfare shows some parallel with his blockchain and cryptocurrency influenced present and future as he speaks on in this episode. He also sheds some light on his contributions to The Declaration of Currency Independence, his opinion on whether fiat and crypto can co-exist and the future of the cryptocurrency space as a whole.
