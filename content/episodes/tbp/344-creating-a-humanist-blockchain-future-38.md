## Episode Description

In this episode of Creating a Humanist Blockchain Future Rhys interviews Primavera De Filippi, the co-author of Blockchain and the Law, an advisor for DAOStack, a faculty associate at Harvard’s Berkman-Klein Center, and a permanent researcher at Paris’ National Center of Scientific Research. They chat about her new book, apply internet regulation to crypto, explore her Plantoid project, and chat about why she advises DAOStack. Enjoy and let him know if you have any feedback!
