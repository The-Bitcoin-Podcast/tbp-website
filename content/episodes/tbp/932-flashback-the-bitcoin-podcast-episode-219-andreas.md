## Episode Description

This is a Flashback Episode, we are going back to Aug 2018 in which Andreas Antonopoulos on his fourth appearance on The Bitcoin Podcast Network. This episode we discuss the wide array of wallets and why you should, or should not, own multiple versions. There’s no “one size fits all” Bitcoin wallet. Wallets come on different platforms with different features, so we felt this was an important topic to dive into. We do discuss CoolWallet S primarily and there is a discount code for our listeners below. In addition, we chatted with Andreas Antonopoulos, who swung by for a fourth time to hang out with us. We’ve watched dozens of his interviews over the years and they still blow our minds. No one else we’ve seen comes close to this level of articulation when it comes to the future of crypto. Strap in!
