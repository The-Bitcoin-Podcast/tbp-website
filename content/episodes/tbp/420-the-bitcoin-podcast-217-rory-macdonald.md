## Episode Description

Stacked episode this week as we invite our ol' pal Kris '#BlockchainBeardGuy' on the roundtable to discuss current events and privacy coins. Kris is passionate about decentralized architecture. He is a Blockchain educator in Denver and is an active advocate for all things Blockchain. Also, Bellator Welterweight Champion, Rory MacDonald, stops by to discuss his crypto journey that begin in 2014, his $250,000 Dash sponsorship and his new Bitcoin.com partnership. The partnership entails two limited edition “Bitcoin Cash Warrior” T-shirts. MacDonald used his Instagram feed to post a picture of himself wearing one of the t-shirts, noting that he was “proud” to launch the t-shirts and that they helped to “celebrate my passion for cryptocurrency.”
