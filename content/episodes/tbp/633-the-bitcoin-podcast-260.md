## Episode Description

The 3 Amigos of Crypto dive into more of the depth that you're used to with them. They go into discuss some scaling issues and what potential affects they can have on cyrptocurrency in general. Also...Coinstar is expanding, is this a sign that adoption to non-crypto GPPs is starting to take hold. Fergulati gets on his high horse about the failure to launch approach to trying to make a blockchain that can do "all the things". Ray Redacted swings by to discuss a perfect opportunity to HODL+, a 120% gain on the BTC price on a 90-day roll! Enjoy!
