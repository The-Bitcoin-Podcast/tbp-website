## Episode Description

Our pal Jon Fitch returns to the show to give us an update on the Ali Act and how his crypto investments have matured over the years. He is now headed to Bellator in an exclusive multi-fight, multi-year deal. He has won his last four fights in a row under the WSOF/PFL banner. The majority of Fitch’s 16-year career has been spent in the UFC. Will he be getting crypto sponsorships in his fights? How does he view the current alt coin scene? It's always good to get a warriors perspective on the show and he even gives us an update on The Legends Room!
