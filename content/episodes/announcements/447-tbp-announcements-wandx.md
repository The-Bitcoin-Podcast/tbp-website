## Episode Description

Today we talk with Abhinav Ramesh from WandX. WandX seeks to establish an infrastructure for decentralized creation and trade of ERC20 Tokens built on the Ethereum blockchain. The pilot application allows users to create combinations of ERC20 Tokens (known as a Basket of Tokens) and trade the Basket by way of a single transaction. Enjoy! Link: https://www.wandx.co/
