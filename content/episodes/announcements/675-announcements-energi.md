## Episode Description

Welcome to Announcements, hosted by Lucian, a show that provides you details on current events, upcoming projects in the blockchain space, and token offering details. Today we have Energi! Being the world’s leading cryptocurrency with the unification of Smart Contracts, Governance & Self-funding Treasury to ensure longevity and enable rapid growth. Energi will adopt all of Ethereum’s existing capabilities, but will also be imbued with their finely tuned Treasury and Governance model to drastically expand growth and adoption. We aspire to be a globally-impacting organization with what we feel are the right ingredients to achieve it, so we chat with them today.
