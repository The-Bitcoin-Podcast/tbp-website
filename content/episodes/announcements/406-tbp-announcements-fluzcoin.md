## Episode Description

Today we talk with Maurice Hararay of Fluzcoin. Fluzcoin is a retail protocol specifically designed to solve the most salient problems within cryptocurrency: speed, cost, compliance, and excess volatility. Fluzcoin, is a participatory and independent retail coin with its foundation based governance, is simultaneously solving the four primary issues nowadays associated with cryptocurrencies: transaction speed, transaction cost, KYC compliance and excess volatility. Fluzcoin thereby creates a true digital retail currency, ready for mass adoption. Enjoy! Link: Fluzcoin.io
