## Episode Description

Welcome to Announcements with Host No. 2, Demetrick Ferguson, a show that provides you details on current events, upcoming projects in the blockchain space, and token offering details. We are attempting to provide you with details and information from the people who are creating projects, and we are not giving advice. Please do proper due diligence when considering investments. MXC is the pioneer IoT Cryptocurrency. Using the MXProtocol we’ve redefined the world of IoT data, enabling secure, vastly scalable, lightning fast transactions. Data is all around us, at MXC we’re delivering the Decentralized Global IoT Data Network. Using Blockchain technology we proudly support a wide range of industries, from Smart Cities to Global Transportation Conglomerates, even down to your local Farmers’ market.
